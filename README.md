# 🛒 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, product categories, weekly and monthly sales trends, and top-performing products based on sales and customer reviews.

It helps users explore product performance and sales metrics through interactive visualizations and filters.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze Amazon product sales performance.
- Track Year-to-Date (YTD) and Quarter-to-Date (QTD) sales.
- Identify top-performing products based on sales and reviews.
- Compare sales performance across product categories.
- Understand monthly and weekly sales trends.
- Support data-driven business decisions through visual analytics.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation, where applicable
- **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations, where applicable
- **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, tables, and KPI cards

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by Month
Visualizes monthly sales trends to identify changes in performance throughout the year.

### 2. Sales by Week
Displays weekly sales performance to help explore fluctuations and patterns.

### 3. Sales by Product Category
Compares YTD sales, QTD sales, and percentage contribution across product categories.

### 4. Top 5 Products by YTD Sales
Highlights the five leading products based on year-to-date sales.

### 5. Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews
Displays the top five products based on review metrics.

### 6. Interactive Filters
Allows users to filter dashboard data by product category and quarter.
